For authors

MXB Facts answers the same questions about every tool, with evidence for each answer, from a published prompt. The questions and the rules are public, so you can see what your tool would get, and change it if you want to, before anyone checks it. Authors aren't told before a card is published, except about a security problem (rule 9); anyone, you included, can correct a card afterwards (how a card is made).

What earns a Good answer

The answer each question needs for a Good mark. A Note or a Concern can be the honest answer for what a tool is for: a mark says what a player should know, and what to change, if anything, is up to you. The cost is what a Concern takes off its area, at most (how scores are worked out).

Once your tool has a card

Everything below is at the foot of your tool's page, under "For the tool's author".

  1. Go through it with your agent: download the card and paste one prompt. Your agent follows each answer's evidence into your code; we don't suggest fixes.
  2. Release, then request a re-check. The new release gets a new card; the old one stays.
  3. Something wrong, missing, or context to add? Use "Comment" beside any answer, Good ones included, or comment on an answer with its evidence; it is answered in public.
  4. Add the badge to your page: your score in five styles, each pinned to the card's version.
