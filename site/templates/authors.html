For authors

MXB Facts answers the same questions about every tool, with evidence for each answer, from a published prompt. The questions and the rules are public, so you can see what your tool would get, and change it if you want to, before anyone checks it. Authors aren't told before a card is published, except about a security problem (rule 9), and from 26 Nov 2026 no author is shown a card before it is published, except the maintainer, who publishes every card, the maintainer's own tool's included. When your card is published, the maintainer sends you a short note with its link, if you have a public contact. Anyone, you included, can correct a card afterwards, on GitHub (how a card is made).

A licence that forbids analysis gets a card of public facts only, and no score ("Not scored"): what couldn't be checked counts neither for your tool nor against it, but players see that nothing inside it could be checked. A clause added in a later release applies from that release on; earlier cards keep their score.

What earns a Good answer

The answer each question needs for a Good mark. A Note or a Concern can be the honest answer for what a tool is for: a mark says what a player should know, and what to change, if anything, is up to you. The cost is what a Concern takes off its area, at most (how scores are worked out).

Once your tool has a card

Everything below is at the foot of your tool's page, under "For the tool's author".

  1. Go through it with your agent: download the card and paste one prompt. Your agent follows each answer's evidence into your code; we don't suggest fixes.
  2. Release, then request a re-check. The new release gets a new card; the old one stays.
  3. Something wrong, missing, or context to add? Use "Comment on …" under any answer's evidence, Good ones included, or the comment form; it is answered in public.
  4. Show the card on your pages: the card image for a mods-site post, a forum or your site (it always shows your newest card), the page's address for Discord, which draws the same image, or a badge for a README, pinned to one release. Each comes ready to copy as HTML, Markdown or BBCode.

Show cards on a mods site or in an app

Every card can be shown without asking us, under {{ SITE.licence.data | replace('-', ' ') }}: credit MXB Facts with a link to the card. Wherever you show a score, show the release it covers and the AI note; the card image has both. The same addresses work for every tool, and the tool's id is the last part of its page's address.
