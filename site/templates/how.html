Where to say what

{# Two verbs for players: Comment when something needs a decision (an issue, answered in public), Discuss when it is talk. #}
Comment
An answer is wrong, something is missing, or you are the author and want to add context: use "Comment" beside the answer, or the comment form. It opens a GitHub issue, the answer shows "Under review" while it is open, and it is closed in public as fixed, declined or noted, with the reason; a fix adds a dated line to the card's history.
Discuss a tool
Talk with other players and the author: each tool has one discussion for all its releases, linked from its page and its card in the tools list (all of them). What is said there changes no card until it comes in as a comment.
Discuss the site
The questions, the rules, how cards are made, an idea, or something broken on the site: the site's discussion. An idea that is taken becomes an issue, and a change to the rules is listed in "Changes to these rules".
Ask for something
A tool analysed, or a new question for every tool: request a tool or suggest a question. Each is an issue, closed with a decision and its reason.
